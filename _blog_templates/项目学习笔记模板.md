BEP relationships[^bep] are used to estimate activation energies, while LOOCV[^loocv] can be used to evaluate predictive performance.

[^bep]: **BEP**：Brønsted–Evans–Polanyi 关系，建立反应能与活化能之间的经验关联。

[^loocv]: **LOOCV**：Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation，留一交叉验证。
